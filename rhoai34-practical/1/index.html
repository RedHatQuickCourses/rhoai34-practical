Module 1: Introduction and Overview

The Shadow AI Crisis

The CIO at your enterprise just called an emergency meeting. She projects a slide showing two deeply concerning charts.

"We have a $1.5 million 'Shadow AI' problem," she begins, pointing to the first chart. "Developers across five different departments are expensing unmanaged, external LLM API keys. We have zero visibility into what data is leaving our network or how many tokens we are consuming."

She points to the second chart. "At the same time, we’ve invested over $500,000 in internal GPU infrastructure. Yet, our data scientists are waiting weeks for capacity because resources are hoarded by single teams or sitting idle."

The business needs to regain control. By centralizing AI operations, enterprises have realized over $5 million in cost avoidance , achieved a 40%+ reduction in GPU hours, and delivered 2x faster model responses.

Your mandate is clear: Transform this fragmented chaos into a secure, governed, and highly scalable Enterprise AI Factory.

Product Overview by Persona

OpenShift AI 3.4 brings massive updates that directly solve our CIO’s crisis, targeting three core roles within the enterprise.

The Platform Engineer (Your Role)

You are responsible for securing the infrastructure, optimizing hardware utilization, and providing visibility into resource consumption.

  • Models-as-a-Service (MaaS) & Observability: Replaces fragmented API keys with a flexible, internal subscription model. Define subscriptions to grant specific groups access to models with strict token quotas, and use the unified dashboard to generate chargeback reports for finance.

  • Distributed Inference (llm-d): Overcomes the lack of single-node GPU capacity for massive models by providing a Kubernetes-native framework to serve large language models at scale across multiple nodes.

  • Kueue for Hardware Sharing: Maximizes your GPU ROI by intelligently managing quotas and prioritizing job scheduling, stopping the "GPU turf wars."

  • MCP Catalog: A centralized UI for discovering and securely deploying Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to prevent unvetted agentic integrations.

The Data Scientist

Focused on discovering the right models, ensuring their accuracy, and optimizing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines.

  • Model Catalog & "Tool Calling" Filter: A curated library of generative AI models. The new "Tool calling" filter instantly identifies models validated for agentic workflows.

  • EvalHub: An evaluation orchestration service that tests LLMs against standardized benchmarks to guarantee safety and compliance.

  • AutoRAG (Technology Preview): Automatically tests combinations of chunking, embedding, and retrieval settings to rank the best configurations for your RAG pipelines.

The Application Developer

Needs frictionless, self-service access to models and tools to build intelligent applications quickly.

  • Gen AI Playground: An interactive chat environment to rapidly prototype foundation models and test external tools before writing any application code.

  • Self-Service API Keys: Developers can instantly generate short-lived temporary API keys for prototyping, or permanent keys tied to their MaaS subscriptions, removing IT bottlenecks.
